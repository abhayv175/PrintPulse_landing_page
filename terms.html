Section 2

Platform Role & Direct Peer-to-Peer UPI Settlement

PrintPulse serves exclusively as a decentralized technical relay architecture designed to streamline document transfer and printing between customers and independent print shop kiosks:

Technical Signaling & Spooling Relay:

PrintPulse performs client-side inspection, server-side PDF page counting, print parameter extraction (copies, page ranges, color vs. black-and-white mode), and real-time WebSocket order dispatch to the vendor's physical terminal. PrintPulse is not a print vendor, publisher, or physical printing service provider.

Direct Peer-to-Peer NPCI UPI Settlements:

All monetary transactions for printing services occur directly between the customer and the print shop vendor via the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) Unified Payments Interface (UPI). The customer pays directly to the vendor's registered merchant VPA or QR code.

Non-Banking & Zero Escrow Status:

PrintPulse is not a payment aggregator, bank, financial institution, or escrow agent. PrintPulse does not hold, handle, touch, or process customer funds, does not levy transaction fees or percentage commissions on print charges, and does not store banking credentials or UPI PINs.

Print Shop Vendor Responsibilities & Authentication

Vendors operating print shops through the PrintPulse Android App or administrative endpoints agree to uphold the following standards:

  • Google OAuth Authentication: Vendors authenticate via Google Sign-In. Vendors are solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their Google account and local session tokens, and for all activities conducted under their vendor identifier (vendor_id).

  • Payment & UTR Verification: Vendors are required to verify that the customer-submitted Unique Transaction Reference (UTR) matches an actual credit in their UPI merchant account before releasing or fulfilling physical print outputs.

Section 5

Automated File Deletion & Ephemeral Architecture

PrintPulse operates an ephemeral-by-design pipeline. When documents are uploaded for printing:

  • Immediate Post-Print Purge: Uploaded files are automatically unlinked and permanently deleted from disk storage immediately following successful print job completion, order rejection, or cancellation.
  • Automatic Expiration: Staged files not acted upon within 24 hours are automatically purged to prevent disk clutter and protect customer privacy.
  • No Archival Warranty: PrintPulse does not provide long-term cloud storage or backup services. Customers are solely responsible for maintaining backup copies of their original documents.
